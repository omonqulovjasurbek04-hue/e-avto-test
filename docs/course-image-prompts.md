# Kurs kartalari uchun rasm promptlari

## Umumiy vizual yo‘nalish

- Maqsad: `E-Avto Test` kurslar katalogidagi kartalar uchun alohida cover rasmlar.
- Uslub: zamonaviy, realistik avtomobil ta’limi fotosurati; tabiiy yorug‘lik; sokin ko‘k, oq va kulrang tuslar.
- Format: gorizontal `1536 × 1024` (kurs kartasida markaz qismi kesilib ko‘rinishini hisobga olish).
- Barcha promptlar uchun qoida: **rasmda matn, harf, raqam, logo, watermark, davlat raqami va tanib bo‘ladigan brend belgisi bo‘lmasin**.
- Kompozitsiya: asosiy obyekt markazda va yuqori o‘rta qismda; pastki chetlarda kurs kartasi uchun xavfsiz bo‘sh joy qoldirilsin.

```text
Asset type: driving-school course-card cover

Scene/backdrop: calm tree-lined city street, subtle road signs in the distance, realistic road markings
Subject: anonymous learner driver’s compact sedan, viewed from front three-quarter angle
Style/medium: polished editorial automotive photography
Composition/framing: horizontal wide crop, car centered in the upper-middle area, room around it for course-card cropping
Lighting/mood: clear soft morning light, confident and welcoming
Color palette: deep blue, clean white, neutral asphalt, soft greenery
Constraints: no visible faces, no readable number plates, no logos, no text, no watermark
Avoid: racing, traffic jam, luxury branding, distorted wheels, unsafe driving
```
